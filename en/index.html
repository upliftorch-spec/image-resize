Frequently Asked Questions

Does resizing reduce image quality?
Downsizing typically preserves quality well. Upscaling may cause blurriness — we recommend not exceeding 200% of the original size.
Are my images uploaded anywhere?
No. All processing happens entirely in your browser. Your images never leave your device, ensuring complete privacy.
What social media presets are available?
We support Instagram Post (1080x1080), IG Story (1080x1920), Facebook Cover (820x312), Twitter Post (1200x675), and more common sizes.
How do I use the crop feature?
Click "Enable Crop", then drag on the image to select your crop area. Click "Apply Crop" to confirm. You can then resize the cropped image further.
Can I resize and convert format at the same time?
Yes. You can resize while choosing the output format (JPG, PNG, WebP) and quality setting simultaneously.
